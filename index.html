      Questions

      Before you buy

      How do I read the cookbook?

      It opens in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Each recipe has an ingredient list you can tick off while you cook.

      Do I need special equipment?

      No. A sheet pan, a large frying pan, a pot for rice or pasta and about 20 containers will cover every recipe.

      How long does the food keep?

      Each recipe says how many days it keeps in the fridge, usually four or five. Recipes that freeze well are marked.

      I'm cooking for one. Will this work?

      Yes. A four-portion batch gives one person lunch for four days. Every recipe halves easily if you want two portions instead of four.
